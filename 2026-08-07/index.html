02

438 sent, four of five days at full capacity

Sending ran at the full 90 on four working days and 78 on the fifth, for 438 across the week and the highest week to date. Every message was written against the live context on the record, reviewed by an automated quality pass, approved before the day opened, and paced across US working hours. Nothing goes out on a day that has not been approved.

03

Bounce reporting is now a standing part of this report

Per the request on the scaling call, deliverability now has its own block above and will appear every week: rate for the week, rate across all sending, addresses retired, and domains dropped before send. This week came in at 1.4 percent against 2.7 percent lifetime, so the trend is downward as the older records work their way out of the base.

04

Fixed the queue so first contacts stop getting crowded out

The daily queue had been filling entirely with follow ups on days when many came due, which left new leads sitting untouched and wasted unused capacity. Priority scoring now carries a ranked reserve list, and any capacity left over after the day's follow ups is filled from it with fresh first contacts. First contacts and follow ups now run side by side every day.

05

Second sending domain finished setup, three more mailboxes in warmup

Three additional mailboxes on a second domain are through setup and in warmup now, which takes daily capacity to 180 in mid August. Six more across two further domains follow, for 12 mailboxes across four domains and 360 a day by late August. Splitting across domains rather than stacking mailboxes on one keeps sending reputation independent per domain.

06

Confirmed the base is now fully covered on first touch

A count against the full base this week showed that first touch coverage is effectively complete. The leads still queued are re engagements of records already contacted at least once, not new names. This is worth stating plainly because it changes what the capacity increase buys: at 360 a day the constraint stops being send volume and becomes supply of names that have never been contacted.

07

Reply handling closes itself out and never double sends

Every reply that lands gets a drafted response with real open times inside the same thread, and the open list clears itself once a thread has actually been answered from our side. A draft sitting unsent does not clear it, so nothing gets answered twice and nothing waiting on us drops off the list.
